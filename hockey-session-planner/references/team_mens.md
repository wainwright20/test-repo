# Men's 1st Team

> Coach: edit the **Defaults** section to match your real squad. The planner uses these whenever your memo doesn't say otherwise.

## Defaults
- Typical numbers: 16 outfield + 2 GKs
- Space: half pitch for blocks 1-3, full pitch for blocks 4-5
- Length: 75 minutes
- Kit: plenty of balls (20+), cones, 3 sets of bibs, 4 mini goals
- Level: adult league 1st team, so assume competent basic skills

## What works with this group
- **Intensity and tempo.** Adult 1st-teamers switch off in slow, static drills. Keep rest short and work-to-rest ratios game-like (e.g. 45-60s on, 30s off in waves).
- **Decision-making over pure technique.** Even in the technical block, add a decision quickly (a defender, a choice of two passes, a trigger).
- **Tactical language.** Use team-shape terms: press triggers, outlets, channels, overloads, switch of play, 3D skills, leads, double leads, base, interception lines, transition.
- **Guided questions.** "Where's the space?" or "What made that press work?" gets more buy-in from adults than being told.
- **Competition.** Keep score in small-sided blocks, give points for the theme behaviour, and play winners against winners.
- **Match prep.** If the memo mentions an upcoming opponent, tie the key messages to that game.

## Theme ideas this group often needs
Pressing and press-breaking, outletting from the back, transition (attack ↔ defence), circle entries and finishing, penalty corners (attack and defence), 1v1 eliminations, aerials (only if the memo asks, with safety notes), defensive channelling and tackling.

## Watch-outs
- Aerials and hard hitting in small areas need safety notes, and GKs must be fully kitted.
- Avoid long queues of 4+. Adults will lose interest.
