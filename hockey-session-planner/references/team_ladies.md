# Ladies' 1st Team

> Coach: edit the **Defaults** section to match your real squad. The planner uses these whenever your memo doesn't say otherwise.

## Defaults
- Typical numbers: 14 outfield + 1 GK
- Space: half pitch for blocks 1-3, full pitch for blocks 4-5
- Length: 75 minutes
- Kit: plenty of balls (20+), cones, 2-3 sets of bibs, 4 mini goals
- Level: adult league 1st team, so assume competent basic skills

## What works with this group
- **Game-realistic decisions.** Adult 1st-team players want to see how a drill transfers to Saturday. Make the "link to theme" clear in every block.
- **Tempo and touch quality.** Passing patterns and rondos with a clear tempo target (one or two touch) work well as warm-ups.
- **Tactical language.** Press triggers, leads and double leads, outlets, channels, overloads, switch of play, 3D skills, interception, transition.
- **Guided questions** and players' ideas: invite the group to solve problems ("How do we beat a 3-player press?").
- **Competition.** Keep score in the small-sided and live blocks.
- **Match prep.** If the memo mentions an upcoming opponent, tie the key messages to that game.

## Planning notes
- If numbers are uneven or the session is light on players, use floaters (`neutral`) instead of standing anyone out.
- One GK is common, so where both ends need a keeper, plan a rotating outfield "kicking back" player or use mini goals at one end.

## Watch-outs
- Aerials and hard hitting in small areas need safety notes, and the GK must be fully kitted.
- Avoid long queues of 4+.
