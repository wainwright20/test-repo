# Minis (U5 - U10)

> Coach: edit the **Defaults** section to match your real set-up (numbers, helpers, how you split ages).

## Defaults
- Typical numbers: 20-30 children across U5-U10, split into age groups
- Helpers: assume 2-3 parent / young-leader helpers so groups can run at the same time
- Space: half or quarter pitch, split into grids
- Length: 75 minutes (keep the coach's structure; shorten blocks only if the memo says so)
- Kit: a ball per child, lots of cones and markers, bibs, mini goals, sized sticks
- No goalkeepers in kit. Small-sided games use mini goals or "dribble over the line" scoring.

## Keep the coach's 5-block structure, made age-appropriate
| Block | What it looks like for minis |
|---|---|
| Warm-up (10) | A tag or chase game that sneaks in the skill, e.g. "Sharks and Minnows" while dribbling, "Traffic Lights" for stopping and turning the ball. |
| Technical (15) | Short demo (under 30 seconds), then **everyone with a ball**. No lines. Stations or "copy the coach" challenges with fun names ("Pirate's treasure", "Hungry crocodiles"). |
| Small-sided (15) | 1v1 / 2v1 games into gates or end lines. Lots of tiny games at once. |
| Live game scenario (15) | 3v3 or 4v4 on small pitches with mini goals, rolling subs, quick restarts. |
| Game (20) | Mini-hockey format for the age band (small teams, small pitch, no GKs). Add one simple "bonus point" rule tied to the theme. |

## Age bands (adapt within the plan when ages are mixed)
- **U5-U6**: fundamental movement plus "ball and stick friends". Very short instructions, lots of imaginative play, 1 ball each. Games end quickly and restart. Avoid complex rules.
- **U7-U8**: dribbling with control, stopping the ball, push passing, simple 1v1. Introduce "team" ideas such as spreading out.
- **U9-U10**: passing and receiving on the move, basic tackling (block tackle), 2v1 decisions, simple positions in small-sided games.

If the session mixes age bands, show in the plan how groups split (e.g. "U5-U7 grid / U8-U10 grid") and give one progression and one regression so helpers can adapt.

## Coaching style for minis
- Coaching points are simple and in child language: "ball on the stick's flat side", "eyes up like a meerkat", "little taps, not big hits".
- Praise effort. Use "freeze" moments sparingly.
- Under-10s concentrate in short bursts, so change the game every 5-7 minutes within a block (a progression counts as a change).
- Nobody stands in a line. If there are queues, add another grid or another ball.

## Safety / welfare
- Stick safety: stick below waist height, no lifting the ball.
- Gum shields and shin guards. Check the space is clear of bags and kit.
- Parents and helpers should be DBS-checked in line with club policy. Keep a register.
